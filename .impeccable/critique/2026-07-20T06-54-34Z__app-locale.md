## Anti-patterns verdict

The local candidate does not look like a generic AI-made interface. It has a coherent policy-journal visual register and strong evidence structures. The copy still contains a recognizable generated-language signature: `画像／基线／相邻／拉向／保持开放／编辑型／有日期快照／相连证据／屏幕轴` recur as direct mappings from English product or model language.

The deterministic detector scanned 55 targeted files and reported three `overused-font` warnings in `components/current-case/current-case.module.css`. Two are false positives because the Chinese print rule overrides them. The remaining `dt { font-family: Arial }` is a real Chinese print fallback risk.

## Overall impression

The editorial architecture is sound; the language needs one native-policy edit and the deployment needs to contain the build being reviewed. The largest copy opportunity is to replace abstract taxonomy and model metaphors with ordinary Chinese policy prose without weakening methodological caveats.

## What is working

- Current Case links each factual statement to sources, preserves original source titles, exposes the evidence window, and provides a correction route.
- Foundation clearly labels the Chinese instrument as an adapted beta and makes no cross-language equivalence claim.
- The map explicitly denies calibrated distance, population density, and statistical similarity; canonical identifiers and geometry remain locale-neutral.
- At 390px in the local candidate, tested pages had no document-level horizontal overflow; Song/Hei hierarchy and Chinese date formatting were sound.

## Priority issues

### P0 — The deployed Chinese edition does not exist

All representative production routes (`/zh`, cases, sources, corrections, map, profiles, Profile, Foundation, AI, Privacy, and Methods) return the same 13,235-byte English 404 with `x-matched-path: /404`.

### P0 — Chinese routes can bypass or duplicate locale routing

- Remove `locale="en"` from the Foundation and Perspective links in `app/[locale]/explore/atlas/page.tsx` and from the empty Profile Foundation link.
- Normalize stored result paths before giving them to the localized `Link`: use `internalPath(foundation.resultPath)` and `internalPath(run.resultPath)` in `lib/field/items.ts`. The current release candidate generated `/zh/zh/results/...`.

### P1 — Evidence and privacy language sometimes overclaims

- `反复援引裁决并未带来可见的行动变化，反而可能削弱裁决本身的权威。` → `反复援引裁决后，尚未观察到明显的行动变化；部分支持方开始担心，仅重复表态会降低其政治效力。`
- `分析请求不附带请求 IP、Cookie、用户代理或来源标头。` → `自定义分析事件不会把 IP 地址、Cookie、用户代理或来源页作为事件属性发送；基础设施提供方仍可能按其政策处理网络请求元数据。`
- Tetlock sentence → `这项研究提醒读者检验单一框架的局限，并根据证据更新判断。`
- Sil/Katzenstein sentence → `它为理解混合结果提供了一个理论参照。`

### P1 — Foundation options have unequal prestige

Replace the complete `tradeoff_strategy` group with parallel title-and-cost constructions:

1. `利用战略窗口` — `如果当前行动有望形成可持续优势，就应把握窗口，即使这会增加承诺和升级风险。`
2. `控制承诺与升级` — `如果继续竞争会导致战略透支，就应限制行动范围，即使对手可能取得局部进展。`
3. `评估国内承载力` — `是否行动，取决于国内政治联盟、公众承受力和财政空间能否长期支持，即使可能错过短期机会。`
4. `巩固技术与产业基础` — `应优先建设技术、产业和供应链能力，即使短期内无法改变力量对比。`

Replace the complete `case_semiconductors` group:

1. `维持关键技术优势` — `对可能改变军事与国家安全能力的技术实施较广限制，即使承担贸易损失。`
2. `降低结构性依赖` — `扩大本国产能、减少对单一来源的依赖，即使短期成本较高。`
3. `协调盟友管制` — `与盟友共同限制高风险技术，以换取一致执行，但接受协调速度较慢。`
4. `限制安全化范围` — `只把直接涉及国家安全的技术差距纳入限制，即使这会允许对手在其他领域继续追赶。`

- Display `TWN` as `台湾地区` on the Mainland Simplified Chinese route only; retain the canonical ID and geometry.
- `中等强国` → `中等力量国家`.
- In the South China Sea case, `域外国家` → `非南海声索国`; `反华阵营标志` → `针对中国的阵营化信号`.

- Use `正当性` for legitimacy and `法律授权` for a legal mandate; avoid `合法授权` where the distinction matters.

## Persona red flags

- Mainland policy professional: `台湾` presented among country labels, `中等强国`, `域外国家`, `反华阵营`, and `经济治国方略` create avoidable terminology risk.
- IR student or researcher: `家族`, `规范画像目录`, `屏幕轴`, `相连证据`, and the inconsistent use of legitimacy/legal authorization blur methodological distinctions.
- Methodology reader: two-decimal display, the Tetlock/Sil claims, and the privacy IP sentence overstate what the method or implementation can support.
- Mobile reader: long case headings can break at weak semantic points; 9.9–11.2px Chinese tags and labels are undersized.

## Minor observations

- The local 390px pages tested had no document-level horizontal overflow.
- Dates are rendered in natural Chinese format and English bibliographic punctuation is appropriately retained inside original titles.
- The separator ` · ` is visually Western but not inherently wrong; prioritize the spaced slash (`44 / 44`) and source-line em dash first.
- `战略对冲` and `互操作性` are valid Mainland IR terms; they need plain-language glosses for general readers, not replacement.
