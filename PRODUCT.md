# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Readers use this editorial interactive to examine how they judge international-relations tradeoffs, compare personal and contextual positions, and understand the evidence behind public reference postures. It must remain useful without prior theory training and must not overstate what the model can infer.

## Product Purpose

The IR Worldview Inventory maps a respondent's answers across seven dimensions, summarizes nearby worldview families, and adds issue modules, contextual Perspective Runs, Decision Patterns, and evidence-coded Reference Profiles. Success means a reader can locate their own judgments, see where context changes them, and understand the model's limits without mistaking its outputs for psychometric facts or fixed identities.

## Positioning

The product combines a scored Foundation result with transparent interpretation, contextual reruns, and source-qualified public positions. Named families and archetypes summarize a continuous profile; they do not assign a natural kind, nationality, culture, public figure, or organization to the reader.

## Operating Context

Readers move between the Foundation questionnaire, review, result, Profile, Perspective Runs, issue modules, Explore field guide, Worldview Map, Decision Pattern pages, reference profiles, and share routes. Navigation is reversible, saved drafts can resume, and issued links must continue to decode through registered compatibility paths.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The Foundation uses seven scored dimensions and resolves a nearby family, strategy modifier, normative modifier, and pure or blended archetype reading.
- The Worldview Map distinguishes the categorical archetype matrix from the secondary continuous projection; the latter does not encode posture.
- Decision Patterns are editorial aids, never assigned user results. Reference positions are dated, scoped evidence snapshots.
- Scoring, payload formats, archetype resolution, stable IDs, and locale boundaries are versioned contracts. Presentation work must not silently change them.
- Nationality, citizenship, and culture never alter scoring. Under-modeled traditions remain explicit.
- Simplified Chinese routes publish only approved localized material; missing content fails closed instead of falling back to English.

## Brand Commitments

The name is **IR Worldview Inventory**. The voice is rigorous, editorial, calm, and concrete: closer to a concise policy-journal interactive than to a SaaS dashboard, AI demo, personality-test funnel, therapy app, lecture note, or dissertation abstract. Copy explains the payoff first, defines necessary terms, and avoids theatrical certainty, fake precision, generic uplift, and polished contrastive slogans.

## Evidence on Hand

- Versioned item, scoring, calibration, payload, and compatibility code lives in the repository with automated validation and replay coverage.
- Evidence-coded reference profiles carry dates, scopes, sources, qualification, and review status.
- The V23.1 English archetype catalog is owner-authorized, AI-assisted editorial-beta copy with human editorial review pending. It is not externally validated or individually expert-reviewed.
- No representative population norms or validated population percentiles are available; the interface must not imply either.

## Product Principles

1. Deliver the interpretive payoff before methodology caveats.
2. Preserve user control through reversible navigation, explicit review, and resumable drafts.
3. Distinguish personal baselines, contextual runs, Decision Patterns, and public reference postures without implying permanent identities.
4. Make evidence, uncertainty, and methodological limits legible without weakening the main result.
5. Prefer typed shared contracts and stable legacy decoding over duplicated labels or fragile payload logic.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal conformance level is claimed. Release acceptance requires keyboard access, semantic-list parity for mapped content, non-color distinctions, visible focus, reduced-motion behavior, a usable 390-pixel layout and 400% reflow, print legibility, and plain recovery from invalid payloads. Nationality, citizenship, and culture must never alter scoring.
