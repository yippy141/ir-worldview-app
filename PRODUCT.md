# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Readers use this editorial interactive to examine how they judge international-relations tradeoffs, compare personal and contextual positions, and understand the evidence behind public reference postures. They need a reflective experience that remains useful without prior theory training and does not overstate what the model can infer.

## Product Purpose

The IR Worldview Inventory maps a respondent's answers across seven dimensions, summarizes nearby worldview families, and adds issue modules, contextual Perspective Runs, and evidence-coded Reference Profiles. Success means the user can locate their own judgments, see where context changes them, and understand the model's limits without mistaking its outputs for psychometric facts or fixed identities.

## Brand Personality

Rigorous, editorial, calm. The voice should resemble a concise policy-journal interactive: direct, concrete, transparent about evidence, and confident without theatrical certainty.

## Anti-references

The product must not resemble a SaaS dashboard, AI demo, personality-test funnel, therapy app, lecture notes, or dissertation abstract. Avoid gradients, neon, glassmorphism, oversized shadows, stacked identical cards, decorative charts, fake precision, self-discovery language, and polished contrastive antithesis.

## Design Principles

1. Deliver the interpretive payoff before methodology caveats.
2. Preserve user control through reversible navigation, explicit review, and resumable drafts.
3. Distinguish personal baselines, contextual runs, and public reference postures without implying separate permanent identities.
4. Make evidence, uncertainty, and methodological limits legible without weakening the main result.
5. Prefer typed, shared contracts and stable legacy decoding over duplicated labels or fragile payload logic.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal conformance level is claimed in the repository guidance. Release acceptance requires keyboard access, semantic list parity for map content, non-color distinctions, a usable 390-pixel layout, visible focus, reduced-motion behavior, and plain recovery from invalid payloads. Nationality, citizenship, and culture must never alter scoring.
