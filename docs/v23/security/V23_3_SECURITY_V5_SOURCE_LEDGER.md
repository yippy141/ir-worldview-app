[v4 source ledger](./V23_3_SECURITY_SOURCE_LEDGER.md). This record narrows that

reviewed.

## Evidence rule

- `OF`, `DP`, `DOC`, and `AA` retain the meanings in the frozen v4 ledger.
- `SAx` is a hypothetical condition stated on the card. It requires no outside
  knowledge and is not presented as a real event or selected actor plan.
- A source ID establishes only the bounded claim described in the historical
  register. It does not determine a correct answer.
- IDs marked unresolved, likely, mutable-without-snapshot, or requiring an
  archive in the v4 ledger are not used for a v5 setup claim.

## Closed v4 provenance blockers

Bank v5 does not use `T04`, `T06`, `I04`, `I07`, `I11`, `I13`, or `U07` for
a setup claim. Their unresolved, likely-match, mutable-page, or archive caveats
remain visible in the frozen v4 ledger. In particular, the replacement Tehran
lens does not rely on `I11` or `I13`, does not assume Iranian responsibility for
an unattributed attack, and explicitly states that no shipping lane or strait is
closed.

This is source/provenance closure only. Human subject-matter, regional,
cognitive, pilot, and psychometric gates remain unperformed and are recorded in
the beta release decision.
