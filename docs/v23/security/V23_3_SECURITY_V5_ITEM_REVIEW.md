claim of human subject-matter review, cognitive testing, psychometric
validation, population evidence, or cross-cultural equivalence. The release
decision and deferred gates are recorded in
[V23_3_SECURITY_V5_BETA_RELEASE_DECISION.md](./V23_3_SECURITY_V5_BETA_RELEASE_DECISION.md).

## Replacement Tehran lens

`iran_tehran_leverage` keeps its version-scoped item identity but is fully
rewritten in bank v5. The v4 wording remains only in the frozen v4 bank.

The v5 scene:

- cites only the exact `I06` and `I10` chronology records;
- marks the decision exercise and actor objective as hypothetical;
- states that no shipping lane or strait is closed;
- states that no maritime attack is attributed to Tehran;
- defines declared sites, fuel-cycle activity, missile-test notification, and
  incident hotline on the card;
- avoids a claim that Tehran commands or caused another actor's operation; and
- presents four parallel reciprocal instruments: staged declared-site access,
  time-bound fuel-cycle limits, specified missile-test notification, and a
  regional incident hotline.

Each option names its mechanism and an accepted bargaining, signaling, or
control cost. No option is labeled moderate, expert, lawful, patriotic,
humanitarian, anti-war, or morally correct. The card remains unscored.

## Ukraine analytic-cost repair

The four `ukraine_ceasefire_stall` signals and option IDs are unchanged. Its
accepted-cost clauses now describe limits of the proposed causal mechanism:

- future-force fear can **overstate** what safeguards can contain;
- pressure-as-leverage can **understate** how pressure widens a bargaining gap;
- issue linkage can **overstate** the need to settle linked disputes first; and
- scaled monitoring can **understate** coordination and boundary problems.

The clauses no longer attribute willingness to accept deaths, damage, or
civilian exposure to the respondent. A regression test asserts the parallel
syntax and that human-severity wording does not covary with the activism signal.
