## Contract and boundaries

`lib/connected/evidence.ts` is an additive evidence contract, separate from the frozen module schema. It retains result ID, instrument bank/scorer/form, copy/language provenance, source kind, question context, first/second choices when available, source visibility and permitted use. Relation rules carry IDs, applicability, evidence and conditions that change the wording. Actor-role judgments are describe-only and excluded from personal-choice relations. Scores never supply reasons or reconstruct transcripts.

Comparison requires matching known bank, scorer, form, language and copy. There is no newly assumed equivalence between instruments or historical forms. Deliberately supplied completion details can fill unknown fields but cannot overwrite issued provenance. A 0.35 gap and midpoint buffer are editorial display rules, not statistical significance or population percentiles. Missing subjects count as neither agreement nor disagreement. No pooled worldview score is computed.

New comparisons parse bounded approved links locally without fetching them. Names, pasted values, corrections and reflections do not enter storage, requests, analytics events or new joint links. “Use my saved result” requires selecting one subject. Historical `?left=...&right=...` links still decode and are cleaned from the address bar, with an explicit notice that their original request already reached the server. Cleanup cannot undo that exposure.

The three relation topics have applicable, contrary, mixed, score-only and unavailable regression cases. These states qualify evidence; they are not public badges of logical inconsistency. A staged Technology release and wider investigation can coexist. Rule-setting, capacity and ownership remain distinct. An erased AI rivalry/cooperation distinction remains unavailable.
