## Map groups and sequence

Blue and red are two fictional groupings. Yellow means outside either grouping in that shot and does not imply an alliance. Muted places are unassigned. Every supported feature has an explicit assignment; the 174-polygon basemap plus small-place points is not described as “every country.” Boundaries and point-size conventions are unchanged. Deliberate inspection reports a place's name and fictional group.

The nine complete states last twelve seconds each; the loop lasts 108 seconds. Camera rotation has a separate 140-second revolution. Pause freezes both clocks. Manual rotation or zoom leaves playback paused until Play; reset changes only the camera.
