## Decisions the PM must record

### 1. Flat response-style gate

Ratify that a valence-balanced bank should make flat yea-saying and flat
nay-saying family-invariant. Replace the stale guide requirement that an all-6
and all-2 respondent receive different families. Preserve the separate test
that different cross-dimension response shapes can change family.

### 2. Hedger sign

Ratify the implementation semantics:

- restraint below midpoint → `+`;
- restraint above midpoint → `-`.

This follows the scored restraint dimension and surrounding archetype prose,
but reverses one sentence in the source specification.

### 3. Archetype names

The owner must select names before broad sharing. Keep codes and scoring
stable. The current review recommends:

- retain Kairos, Shi (勢), Concert, Nemawashi (根回し), and Dependencia, with
  the documented caveats;
- decide whether Nomos becomes Charter;
- replace Mandala with Norm Shaper or Legitimacy Shaper;
- decide whether Dirigisme becomes Structural Strategist or receives a
  narrower gloss.

This is an owner decision, not authority for an implementation agent to rename
the types silently.

### 4. Simplified Chinese release contract

Choose one explicit scope:

1. V22 brings Chinese to archetype-first result, live-map, and custom-card
   parity; or
2. the project approves a narrower Chinese contract and continues to omit
   unsupported custom-card/live-map content.

Do not fill missing localized content with silent English fallback.

### 5. Tier 1 activation contract

Keep `TIER1_AGGREGATES_ENABLED` off until the PM records decisions and evidence
for:

1. platform rate limiting, monitoring, and alert ownership;
2. retry/idempotence semantics without a persistent respondent identifier;
3. locale and copy version fixed at quiz start and preserved through resume or
   language switching;
4. an archetype-mapping version in the aggregate cohort contract;
5. staging Postgres execution and catalog/data inspection for both a fresh
   migration `001` schema and a pre-cohort upgrade through migration `003`;
6. production migration execution, clean cohort start, and rollback;
7. opt-out, same-site/proxy, low-sample, malformed-database, and
   unavailable-database smoke tests.

The aggregate schema must remain free of raw answers and persistent respondent
or session IDs. If safe idempotence cannot satisfy that boundary, accept
bounded aggregate overcount risk or redesign the write contract explicitly;
do not smuggle identity into Tier 1.

The SQL definitions and focused static test now cover source-level
fresh/upgrade convergence. That closes the source defect only; it does not
substitute for the live Postgres gate above.

### 6. Replay activation contract

The V1 scorer is frozen, and replay now enforces explicit matching consent,
exact supported V1/V2 item bank, scoring source, mode and form, complete known
item sets, one valid answer representation, and nonzero exit on quarantine
unless the operator supplies `--allow-quarantined-sessions`.

The pipeline remains dormant. Before any production run, require:

1. bounded batches instead of whole-corpus loading;
2. dry-run output plus an operator rollback/reconciliation plan;
3. a reviewed compatibility map before any cross-bank V1↔V2 comparison;
4. production-schema evidence for the consent join and derived-result upsert;
   and
5. an explicit decision on partial progress, because a failed quarantine run
   may already have idempotently upserted valid sessions.

Tier 2 is deferred. Do not use replay code as a reason to introduce raw-answer
storage during V21 closeout.

### 7. Public-sharing decision

Record a separate public-share approval after:

- final archetype names and glosses are locked;
- X, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, and WeChat previews are inspected; and
- the supported Chinese share scope is explicit.

The four final local card renders have already closed the CJK font/layout
check. They do not substitute for real platform previews. A merged PR is not
equivalent to public-share approval.

## V22 product scope

The sprint contract defers four substantive workstreams:

1. hierarchy/authority construct and item coverage;
2. threshold/irreversibility construct and item coverage;
3. Tier 2 raw-answer storage with explicit opt-in and first-party controls;
4. adaptive item selection.

New dimensions must precede new families or archetype codes. Tier 2 must not be
bundled into measurement changes. Adaptive selection must preserve an
auditable, versioned exact-form contract.

## Recommended phase order

### Phase 0: merge and release-decide V21

- Merge PR #24 after the final documentation-only CI run passes.
- Record naming, platform-preview, and Chinese-scope decisions before broad
  public sharing.
- Keep Tier 1 off unless its separate activation checklist passes.
- Keep scoring replay dormant until its operations checklist passes.
- Preserve the exact V2 calibration contracts; do not pool targeted pairs or
  languages for convenience.

Acceptance:

- PR #24 is merged without reopening an accepted runtime gate;
- public-share scope and Chinese limitations are recorded;
- Tier 1 and replay are either explicitly dormant or activated through signed
  checklists.

### Phase 1: specify hierarchy and threshold constructs

- Define each construct, rival explanations, and discriminant validity.
- Draft valence-balanced coverage with at least 40% explicit reverse coding
  per dimension.
- Show how each differs from the existing seven dimensions.
- Pre-register validation, stopping, and translation-equivalence rules.

Acceptance:

- no extreme/compromise/opposite-extreme option structure;
- item coverage exists before a family or archetype is named;
- attainable ranges and synthetic-diagnostic limits are documented.

### Phase 2: validate before adding families

- Pilot the two constructs without emitting new public identities.
- Test within-language and cross-language behavior separately.
- Establish what evidence would justify a new scoring version.

Acceptance:

- no nationality, citizenship, or culture scoring adjustment;
- no pooled-language calibration without equivalence evidence;
- no population inference from synthetic diagnostics.

### Phase 3: extend scoring by version

- Add a new scoring version without modifying V1 or V2.
- Add immutable replay fixtures and all required provenance.
- Prove every modeled family is reachable without an end-of-function override.
- Preserve legacy share identity.

Acceptance:

- strict discrimination tests run with zero skips;
- no score is printed on an unattainable scale;
- exact forms and adaptive policy versions round-trip.

### Phase 4: add hierarchy/threshold presentation mappings

- Add H/T codes only after Phase 3 acceptance.
- Complete owner naming review before public copy.
- Confirm all pure and blended combinations are reachable and legible.

### Phase 5: Tier 2 and adaptive selection

- Design Tier 2 as a separate explicit opt-in flow with retention, access,
  deletion, export, and pseudonymity language.
- Version adaptive policies and record the exact selected items.
- Keep Tier 1 aggregate-only and Tier 2 respondent-level storage logically and
  operationally separate.

## Non-goals and guardrails

- No nationality, citizenship, or culture adjustment to scoring.
- No population-percentile claim from synthetic diagnostics.
- No confidence, clarity, or settledness metric.
- No new family before item coverage.
- No hidden scorer override.
- No pooled English/Chinese calibration without equivalence evidence.
- No raw-answer collection through Tier 1.
- No automatic quiz advance.
- No modification of historical V1 or current V2 merely to simplify V22.
