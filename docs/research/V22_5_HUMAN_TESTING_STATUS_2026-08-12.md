# V22.5 human-testing status

**Decision date:** 12 August 2026  
**Release position:** evidence-ready beta baseline

No human usability round was completed for V22.5. Friends and family were not
repeatedly recruited during daily iteration, and this record must not be read
as implying that any human check occurred.

Automated tests, deterministic fixtures, AI reviews, and visual checks are
pretesting. They are useful for detecting implementation and presentation
failures, but they are not substitutes for human evidence.

Human testing is deferred to one consolidated round after the next major
user-facing integration. That round must occur before broader promotion or
controlled Tier 1 recruitment. The planned round is convenience-sample
usability testing: it may identify comprehension and interaction problems, but
it will not establish validity, reliability, representativeness, or
cross-cultural equivalence.

V22.5 may merge as an evidence-ready beta baseline without claiming human
validation. The cognitive-interview pack remains the protocol for the later
round; it is not evidence that the round has already happened.
