## Delivered behavior

Nineteen published entries, with one optional deeper exercise. All twelve inherited IDs, original names, former project aliases, source identities and legacy fragments remain accessible. Earlier project summaries are preserved in a clearly labeled disclosure on inherited detail pages. The historical twelve-point map remains optional and never contributes to matching.

The complete path works without visiting Departure: browse all futures, compare any two, answer twelve general preference questions, review before submission, inspect several explained possibilities or a qualified non-result, and optionally assess all nineteen scenarios by 2100. General answers do not assume ASI, space travel or a loved one's decision. Personal participation, other people's choices and common rules have distinct scope. Uncertainty, no preference and neither supplied position are complete answers.

Each entry records practical authority, distribution, human/digital status, technical premises, attractions, costs, unknowns, two or three comparisons and source status. Shared feature descriptors are authored separately from prose and map positions. They include present, absent, variant-dependent, unspecified and inapplicable. Catastrophic extinction does not instantiate preferred institutions or valued succession; it supplies no positive preference support, while known conflicts and its availability for expectations remain.

The seven project entries are The Departure, Constitutional Delegation, Human–Machine Federation, Compute Oligarchy, Digital Citizenship, Negotiated Ceiling and Planetary Restoration Compact. Authority Without Understanding remains a cross-cutting failure condition in authority and dependence questions, rather than an extra endpoint. Federation concerns separate polities; Digital Citizenship concerns standing within institutions. Constitutional Delegation establishes revisable administrative authority; Negotiated Ceiling establishes revisable restrictions; Planetary Restoration adds representation of nonhuman ecological interests. They can overlap, and no count of exclusive destinies is implied. Departure variants remain inside its exercise.

## Matching and expectations

`futures-preferences-0.1` compares explicit directional preferences in eight domains with equal active-domain budgets. Agreement contributes one, conflict minus one, and unresolved/inapplicable conditions zero without reallocating their share. Contributions within a domain are averaged. A directly confirmed non-negotiable conflict excludes an entry. An unresolved requirement stays unconfirmed.

A shortlist requires priorities in at least three domains, known features in at least three and at least half of the active domains, support in at least two domains, and positive net support. The first three qualifying entries and all ties at the cutoff are retained. Alphabetical tie display does not express a preference. Catastrophic endpoints cannot qualify for an institutional preference shortlist. These are deterministic editorial rules, not a validated trait model, identity label, population norm or forecast.

Expectations have their own state and explicit horizon, the end of 2100, without assuming ASI occurs. Plausible, unlikely, unsure and unassessed are distinct; overlapping worlds are not normalized into probabilities. Preferences never populate expectations, and expectations never change preference ranking. Identical synthetic Departure answers paired with different general preferences produce different general interpretations.

## Sources and limits

The [FLI aftermath-scenario reference](https://futureoflife.org/ai/ai-aftermath-scenarios/) was reopened to check all twelve source meanings. Original concepts are attributed to Max Tegmark, *Life 3.0* (2017), chapter5. Short paraphrases distinguish the original premises from this project's accounts of life, institutions and feature descriptors. Protector God's feeling of control is not treated as actual human governing authority; Egalitarian Utopia does not receive an invented human-control guarantee. No inherited scenario receives an inferred exit guarantee.

New institutional arrangements are labeled project-authored hypotheticals, with unresolved premises visible. Attribution does not imply permission, endorsement or original invention. Unsupported historical current-development and community-attribution claims remain withheld. No scientific headline was used to justify a catalogue entry's arrival or likelihood. The earlier Departure claim ledger is preserved; this catalogue pass makes no new claim that its fictional technologies are feasible.
